Safety & Advisories

  • ●State Department: Level 1 - Exercise normal precautions. Ireland is generally safe. Petty crime (pickpocketing, purse snatching) is common in tourist areas. Stay aware in crowded spots.
  • ●CDC: No travel health notices for Ireland at this time.
  • ●Weather: June averages 55-65°F, frequently cloudy with rain likely on some days.

Local Tips

  • ●Passport: Ireland does not require 6 months past return, but Atlas keeps the 6-month doctrine and will re-check at T-14.
  • ●Plug: Type G (UK/Irish three-pin), 230V. The Continental Europlug will not fit.
  • ●Visa: Not required for U.S. citizens. ETIAS not required for Ireland (non-Schengen).
  • ●Currency: EUR. Credit card works almost everywhere. Pull small cash for pub tips and market stalls.
  • ●Transit: Leap Card covers Luas, Dublin Bus, and DART. Most of central Dublin is walkable.
  • ●Tipping: 10-15% for sit-down restaurants. Round up at pubs.
  • ●Phrase: "Slainte" (slawn-cheh) for "cheers" at the pub. English is the working language.
